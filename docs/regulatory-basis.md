Validated September 2026. This register anchors architecture discovery; compliance and legal owners must confirm applicability for the licensed entity and operating model.

## Assumptions requiring confirmation

- The legal entity will hold or operate under the necessary banking and payment participation arrangements.
- Scheme certification, message profiles, limits, cut-offs and settlement sponsorship are agreed with relevant participants/providers.
- Hosting location and cross-border data transfers are approved through the bank's legal, privacy and prudential governance.
- Record retention is configured per record category rather than one global period.
- Credit products undergo responsible-lending, affordability, conduct and model-risk review.
